## Problem

The 3 corpus oracles still dropped after Step 40 (jsoup 2, cryptofs 1) are `assertInstanceOf(Type.class, value)`: the
tokens stage failed with "Unsupported assertion type: ASSERT_INSTANCE_OF". The lexer has the token `ASSERT_INSTANCE_OF`
and `JUnitAssertionType` the constant, but the parser had no rule and `parseSnippet` no case. `assertThrowsExactly` had
the same gap (token and constant, no rule; the normalization already handles its lambda blocks,
`AssertionLambdaBlockClassifier`): no corpus oracle, but its oracles would be dropped in the datasets and fail at
inference.

## Fix

Two rules, with the signatures of JUnit 5 (the message a `String` or a `Supplier<String>`, as the other rules since
Step 28):

```
assertInstanceOfMethodCall
    : ASSERT_INSTANCE_OF '(' expression ',' expression (',' (supplierExpression | expression))? ')' SEMI ;
assertThrowsExactlyMethodCall
    : ASSERT_THROWS_EXACTLY '(' expression ',' executable (',' (supplierExpression | expression))? ')' SEMI ;
```

`assertThrowsExactly` is `assertThrows` without the JUnit 4 form (a message first): JUnit 4 has no
`assertThrowsExactly`. The parser is regenerated with the jar of Steps 27–30 (SHA-1 `7df86c34…`); regenerating the
grammar before the change gave the committed files byte for byte. The large diff of `JavaParser.java` is the
renumbering of the rules after the two insertions.

- `AssertionGrammarTest.assertThrowsExactlyHasARule`: the type, the body of a lambda, a method reference, a message.
